## Pipe-copy investigation and instructed repair

The user-supplied source in evidence 35 confirms countfree increments its count through one pipe byte per touched page, while its parent accepts EOF and waits without inspecting child status. An unexpected pipe-write/read failure can therefore truncate the count. pipewrite holds its spinlock while calling copyin; its short-write and closed-reader branches can both produce the helper's diagnostic. An earlier reader copy failure is also a possible path. No post-failure memstat was supplied before QEMU was terminated.

The instructed kernel repair stages writer data in a bounded PIPESIZE stack buffer before acquiring the pipe lock and stages each reader byte before releasing the lock for copyout. Sleep/wakeup and connection checks remain around pipe-buffer manipulation; user copies can perform swap-in outside the spinlock. The source test and its free-count comparison remain in place. The piperead body was not supplied; the instructions provide a complete replacement with the existing signature. Neither save/build success nor the original root cause/absence of leaked pages is confirmed yet. ## Design limits to include in the report

- One CPU: the design has no cross-CPU TLB shootdowns or general multi-CPU eviction synchronization.
- Only eligible private user pages are evicted. Shared pages and mappings marked COW are skipped; even a now-single-owner COW mapping remains ineligible until write handling restores its normal mapping. Fork-heavy workloads can exhaust resident RAM.
- FIFO searches the entire frame table for each victim; this is a simple teaching implementation.
- The reserved swap area is fixed at 16 MB. Allocation can fail when eligible RAM/swap capacity is exhausted.
- The guide's initial prefault approach does not pin buffers for the whole syscall; a later locked pipe copy can fail after eviction. Kernel staging now has both focused pipe1/countfree and complete-suite pass evidence after the repair. Disk swap-in still cannot sleep while holding spinlocks elsewhere.

These are design limits from the guide/supplied implementation. The countfree regression now has a practical repair plus focused and full pass evidence; the precise original failure branch remains untraced. Preserve single-run policy-measurement, residual-slot, and configuration-evidence limitations separately.
